Case study: group balances shown too high

Problem. Some savings groups were shown far more money than they held, and could request loans the group couldn't cover.

Cause. The database view that calculates each group's balance only deducted loans still waiting to be paid. Once a payout was confirmed, the loan dropped out of the sum.

Fix. I corrected the view after confirming that no other view, routine or trigger depended on it, and kept the original definition so the change could be reversed.

Result: balances matched the real money, roughly ten times lower in the worst case, with no transaction records changed.

Also

  • SQL audits of the production database that traced approved loans never paid out and a payout timeout that made successful payments look failed.
  • Findings written up as reports for the team, with the queries behind every figure and a backup before any change.

Case study: correct PINs rejected at login

Problem. After an update, most members couldn't log in, even with the right phone number and PIN.

Cause. The server returned an empty wallet field for most groups. The app treated it as required and crashed, and a catch-all handler showed the crash as "Invalid Phone Number or Password".

Result: making the field optional restored logins, confirmed on a live test group.

Case study: PIN reset that can't be abused

Problem. Members who forgot their PIN had no way back in from the app, and the existing reset endpoint only asked for a phone number, so anyone could keep resetting someone else's PIN and burn SMS credit.

Fix. I added a Forgot PIN button to the app (release 1.0.4), kept the USSD route (enter 00 at the PIN prompt), and limited resets on the server to one every 5 minutes and three a day per member. Every request is logged against the right member, and if the limit check ever fails the reset still goes through, so genuine members are never blocked.

Result: members can reset their own PIN from the app or USSD, with the nuisance capped. I documented the deployment and rollback for the backend team.

Also

  • Kept the Retry button on failed loan payouts until the money is actually paid out.
  • Gave group officers (chair, secretary, treasurer) admin rights and moved the app to the new brand palette.
